Bridging Taiwan and Silicon Valley since 2003.

TAITA-SV was founded on January 3, 2003 in Santa Clara, California to strengthen collaboration between Silicon Valley and Taiwan across industrial, scientific, and technological development and investment.

Today we accelerate deeptech commercialization by connecting Silicon Valley market access and capital with Taiwan's world-class engineering and semiconductor ecosystem — and we help companies find the hidden talent traditional channels overlook.

Vision

A trusted platform and close-knit community where Silicon Valley and Taiwan collaborate deeply — sharing knowledge, forming enduring partnerships, and accelerating innovation through real execution.
